## What English does well

- **Redundant** — lose half of it, recover the meaning
- **Self-repairing** — a puzzled look and you say it differently
- **Expressive** — negotiate meaning you had no words for

None of these survive the next slide.

# Simplex

---

## One carrier. One speaker.

Press to transmit and you **cannot hear**.

So you cannot know you were stepped on.

Neither can the other station.

---

## And then

- No face. No raised eyebrow. No back-channel.
- Band-limited: roughly 300 Hz to 3 kHz.
- The listener is often tired, often not a native speaker, often busy.
- So are you.
- One shot. A clearance acted on wrongly is an aeroplane somewhere it should
  not be. Now.

---

## The response was not "speak better English"

It was to build a **much smaller language** and make it mandatory.

---

## Four moves

1. **Closed vocabulary** — a few hundred phrases, one meaning each
2. **Fixed word order** — callsign, instruction, constraint
3. **Mandatory readback** — build an acknowledgement out of the message
4. **Reserved words** — forbidden except in one exact context

---

{/* _class: quote */}

Every one of the four gives up expressiveness on purpose.

Worse at almost everything. Harder to get wrong at one thing.

---

## What a transmission looks like

```atc
TWR      Speedbird one two three, wind two four zero degrees one two knots,
         runway two seven left, cleared to land.
BAW123   Cleared to land, runway two seven left, Speedbird one two three.
```

Read it as a **pair to be checked against each other** — not as two people
talking.

---

## The claim this course makes

Phraseology is usually taught as a list that has always been there.

It hasn't.

Every phrase was **chosen**, by identifiable people, out of alternatives, on
evidence that was sometimes good and sometimes a single accident.

---

## So for every rule

- What problem was it for?
- What was the alternative?
- What evidence decided it?
- **Does the evidence still hold?**

That last one is live. Week 10 is a technology that removes mishearing
entirely — and takes something else with it.

---

## The accident record

Not here for drama.

It is the only evidence available about where a language engineered against
misunderstanding still lets misunderstanding through.
