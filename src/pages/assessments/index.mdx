Four components. A small early drill to establish you can operate the thing
the rest of the course argues about, a transcript analysis, the audit that
takes the reasoning outside aviation, and ten minutes on a live frequency.

<WeightBar />

They sum to 100, and that is checked rather than asserted:
`spec/assessment-weights.test.ts` fails the suite if it stops being true. The
content schema already verifies that the criteria *within* a weighted
component sum to 100; nothing in the platform checks across components, so
this course checks it.

## What none of them mark

Accent, fluency, speed, and how much you sound like a pilot. Not a criterion
anywhere, and not commented on.

The reasoning is [week 8](/lectures/week-08/)'s: a rating scale for human
language has consequences that do not fall evenly, and this course is not
qualified to apply one to a first-year student. The person who assesses that
professionally teaches week 8 and agrees.

Asking for a repeat counts in your favour. A fluent readback of something you
did not understand is the failure mode, and the
[frequency check](/assessments/frequency-check/) is built around exactly that
distinction.
