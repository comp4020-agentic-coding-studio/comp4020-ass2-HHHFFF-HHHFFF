`SAY AGAIN` — repeat all, or the following part, of your last transmission.

Whatever you asked for, this station did not copy it. The page does not exist,
or it has moved since you last looked.

That is the correct response to not understanding something, and it is the
cheapest two words in the language. [Week 12](/lectures/week-12/) argues that
the cheapness is the whole point: a system where admitting you did not
understand costs you nothing is a system that finds out about
misunderstandings while they are still cheap.

So: say again. Try the navigation above, or search the site.

If you followed a link from somewhere else and it brought you here, the page it
pointed at is gone — start from the [home page](/), or go straight to the
[glossary](/glossary/).
